# Phase 11 — Interview Craft

**Week 14 (second half) · ~6 hrs, then continuous**

---

## Why this phase exists

> ⭐ *"Knowledge you can't deliver in 45 minutes is worth nothing in a round. **This phase is pure ROI.**"*

⭐ **You have eleven phases of knowledge, most of it measured on real infrastructure.** This phase is about
whether any of it comes out of your mouth in the right order, in 45 minutes, while drawing.

⭐ **It is a different kind of phase: nothing here is technical.** Every item is a behaviour, and every one
is drillable. ⭐ **The failure patterns in particular cost NOTHING to fix** — no extra knowledge, no extra
preparation — which makes them the cheapest points in the entire roadmap.

⚠️ **And it cannot be learned by reading.** ⭐ **The lab — recording one 45-minute design and watching it
back — is worth more than every note in this folder.** Do it first if you only do one thing.

---

## How to work this phase

| Step | Do this | Time |
|---|---|---|
| ⭐ **1** | ⭐ **[`lab/`](lab/) — record ONE session TODAY.** Before reading anything | ~90 min |
| 2 | Read [`notes/`](notes/) 01 → 11. Reference half, once | ~1.5 hrs |
| 3 | Fill in the "In your own words" halves — ⭐ **out loud, since that's the skill** | ~1.5 hrs |
| 4 | ⭐ **Record a second session** and compare the scores | ~90 min |
| 5 | [`GATE.md`](GATE.md) — ⭐ **which is a scored recording, not a Q&A** | ~90 min |

> ⭐ **Step 1 comes first deliberately.** Reading the notes first gives you a checklist you'll perform
> self-consciously. ⭐ **Recording first gives you a BASELINE and shows you which notes you actually need** —
> and the gap between the two recordings is the phase's real output.

## Lab

[`lab/`](lab/) — ⭐ **three tools whose only purpose is to remove every excuse not to record yourself.**

| | |
|---|---|
| ⭐ [`drill.js`](lab/drill.js) | **A 45-minute beat timer.** Calls each beat with prompts and time remaining, warns a minute before each ends, and finishes with eight review questions. `node drill.js --list` for a 25-problem bank |
| ⭐ [`SELF-REVIEW.md`](lab/SELF-REVIEW.md) | **A 78-point rubric** — content (62), delivery (16), and the ten failure patterns as penalties. With a session log, because ⭐ **the trend matters more than any score** |
| ⭐ [`SESSION-TEMPLATE.md`](lab/SESSION-TEMPLATE.md) | **The one-page summary**, including ⭐ **the building-blocks checklist that makes practice compound** |

```bash
node lab/drill.js --speed 60     # ~45 seconds, to see the shape
node lab/drill.js                # the real thing
```

## Definition of done

- [ ] ⭐ **You have recorded and watched back at least TWO 45-minute designs**
- [ ] Both are scored in [`lab/SELF-REVIEW.md`](lab/SELF-REVIEW.md), and ⭐ **the second scored higher**
- [ ] All 11 notes have the "In your own words" half filled in — **out loud**
- [ ] ⭐ **You can recite the eight beats with their time budget**
- [ ] ⭐ **You can recite the ten failure patterns, and you know which ones YOU do**
- [ ] ⭐ **You reached the failure-modes beat, unprompted, in both recordings**
- [ ] You cited at least one number you measured yourself in a lab
- [ ] ⭐ **You committed to a recommendation instead of hedging**
- [ ] [`GATE.md`](GATE.md) passed — ⭐ **which is a scored recording, not a Q&A**
